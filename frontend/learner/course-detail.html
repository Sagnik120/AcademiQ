Advanced Quantum Mechanics

Explore the mathematical formalism of quantum theory, state vectors, Hermitian operators, entanglement, and the foundational algorithms powering quantum computation.

Course Curriculum

Module 1: Mathematical Foundations

3 Lectures • 45 Mins

Hilbert spaces, Dirac bra-ket notation, and inner product spaces.

Module 2: Entanglement & Bell States

4 Lectures • 60 Mins

Quantum correlations, EPR paradox, Bell inequality violations, and quantum teleportation.

Module 3: Quantum Circuit Models

5 Lectures • 75 Mins

Hadamard gates, Phase gates, CNOT, and Deutsch-Jozsa algorithm implementation.

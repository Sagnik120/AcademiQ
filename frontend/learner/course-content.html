In this session, we establish the mathematical formulation of a two-level quantum system (qubit). Any pure state can be expressed as a linear superposition of orthogonal basis states |0⟩ and |1⟩.

|ψ⟩ = α|0⟩ + β|1⟩, where |α|² + |β|² = 1
